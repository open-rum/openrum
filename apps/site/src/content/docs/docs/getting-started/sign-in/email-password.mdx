Email and OpenRUM password sign-in is always available. It does not depend on Google, GitHub, OIDC, LDAP, or the external-provider encryption key.

<Steps>

1. ## Create and keep the local Owner

   During the [first production run](/docs/getting-started/production-deployment/first-run/), open `/setup`, enter the Bootstrap Token, and create the Owner with a work email and a password of at least 12 characters. Store those credentials in your recovery vault and verify that they can sign in. An already initialized Instance cannot repeat setup.

1. ## Sign in

   Open the Console login page. Enter the email address and OpenRUM password, then select **Sign in**. The form remains available even when external providers are disabled. If a session expires, sign in again to return to the page you were viewing.

1. ## Set a password for an external account, if needed

   After your external account has access, open **Account → Set OpenRUM password**, enter and confirm a password of at least 12 characters, and save it. You can then use email and this password to sign in locally. Set it before an Instance administrator role is granted; the same password is used for five-minute re-verification of sensitive actions.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Recovery path">
  If an external provider stops working, sign in with the local Owner account and open **System
  Settings → Authentication** to disable or repair the provider. Keep the Owner password available
  independently of your identity provider.
</Aside>
